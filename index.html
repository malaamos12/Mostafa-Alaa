Fitness & wellness

Six trainers, one testing system

I helped six trainers find which offer and which audience really bring customers. A chat cost anywhere from under EGP 2 to over EGP 50, so testing mattered more than spending more.

I tested offers (50% off, 3-month plans, Ramadan, Eid), formats (poster, carousel, video), lookalike audiences, and Facebook against Instagram.

EGP 23K spent, 1,171 chats started and 56K post interactions (Nov 2024 – Nov 2026).
